What happens next

  1. Tap Install on the iOS prompt, then go to your Home Screen. CertSigner appears there while it installs.
  2. If iOS says the developer is not trusted: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap your certificate, then Trust.
  3. If it says “Unable to Install”: this iPhone's UDID is probably not in your profile, or the certificate was revoked.
  4. Open CertSigner. It confirms which certificate signed it, and from then on it re-signs itself.

Good to know

      
What do I need?
A signing certificate exported as .p12 with its password, and a .mobileprovision profile made for that certificate. For a development or ad hoc profile, this iPhone's UDID must be in it.

      
Does the bundle ID change?
With a wildcard profile CertSigner keeps its own bundle ID. With a profile for one specific App ID, CertSigner is signed with that ID so iOS accepts it.

      
What if my certificate was revoked?
Signing still works here, because a browser can't ask Apple. iOS checks when you open the app and will refuse to launch it.

      
Privacy
This page has no accounts, analytics, cookies or third-party code. It stores nothing about you. The temporary install link holds only the signed app, for at most 10 minutes (2 minutes after iOS downloads it), and its contents are never logged.
